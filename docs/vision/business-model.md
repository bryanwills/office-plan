# Business Model

**Status:** Draft, 2026-11-12. Nothing here is priced, launched, or committed.
**Owner:** Bryan Wills
**Scope:** How the assistant, the hardware, and the service make money under
the Kentucky LLC, how part of it goes back to the neurodivergent community, and
what has to happen before anything is sold.

---

## Goals

1. Help neurodivergent people run their lives with less friction.
2. Give back to the ND community in money, time, and attention.
3. Make enough to be self-employed, on a timeline that does not depend on one
   product hitting.

These are in priority order. A revenue idea that works against goal 1 (guilt
mechanics, dark patterns, selling data) is off the table.

---

## Three legs: app, hardware, service

| Leg | What is sold | Why people would pay | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| App | The assistant on web, phone, watch, and desktop | Daily help with capture, first steps, re-entry, and briefs | Free core plus a paid tier; exact split decided after real usage data |
| Hardware | Push-to-talk wearables, desk units, NFC routine kits | Capture without pulling out a phone; physical routines that work when memory does not | Kits and devices sold as one-time purchases |
| Service | Hosted private inference, setup help for self-hosters, and possibly coaching partnerships | People want the privacy of local AI without running a GPU box | Runs on the same stack documented on this site |

Content (the blog, case studies, build logs, and possibly paid deep-dive
guides) supports all three legs and is its own small income stream.

## Giving back

A stated share of profits, time, and attention goes back to the ND community.
Concretely, once there is revenue to share:

- A fixed percentage of profit to ND research and support organizations,
  published on the site each year.
- Free or discounted access for people who cannot afford it.
- Early testing open to the local ND community, with testers credited (with
  their permission) and their feedback logged.
- Open-sourcing parts of the stack that help others build their own tools.

The exact percentage and recipients are open decisions. Write them down here
when they are set so the promise is on record.

## Structure

- One Kentucky LLC holds everything. Formation status and the name decision are
  tracked under [Formation](../formation/entity-decision.md).
- Each app or product line can run under its own domain as a DBA of the LLC.
- The ND assistant brand is kept separate from the stack name (OpenJarvis) and
  from the LLC name. "Neuro Inclusion AI" is the current candidate brand for the
  assistant line, not a decision.
- Once the name is set, claim matching handles across social, writing, and
  sponsorship platforms before the product site launches.

---

## Before anything is sold

1. The LLC exists, with an EIN and a business bank account.
2. The product has a name that is clear to use (trademark search done).
3. A plain-language privacy policy and terms exist, reviewed by someone
   qualified.
4. Hardware has the required radio authorization and battery shipping plan.
5. Every claim on the product site links to a benchmark or case study.
6. Support and refund process is written down and tested once.
7. Pricing is set from real usage and cost data, not guessed.
