# Articles of Organization (Kentucky)

## Filing details

| Item | Value |
|---|---|
| Filing agency | Kentucky Secretary of State — via [Kentucky Business One Stop](https://onestop.ky.gov) |
| Filing fee | **$40** (domestic LLC), same fee online/mail/in-person |
| Processing time | Same day to next business day for online filings |
| Expedited processing | Not offered — Kentucky doesn't have an expedite option |

## Required information

- [ ] LLC legal name (see [Entity & Name Decision](entity-decision.md)) — run a free name-availability search at `sos.ky.gov` first
- [ ] Principal office address
- [ ] Registered agent name + Kentucky physical address (see below)
- [ ] Management structure — member-managed vs. manager-managed
- [ ] Organizer name(s)

## Registered agent decision

A Kentucky LLC must have a statutory agent with a physical KY address, available during business hours. That address becomes part of the public record.

- **Self as agent (free)** — puts your home address (Spring Mill, KY) on the public record
- **Paid registered agent service (~$99/yr)** — keeps your address private

## Filing checklist (in order)

1. [ ] Confirm LLC name availability (free, sos.ky.gov)
2. [ ] Decide registered agent (self vs. service)
3. [ ] File Articles of Organization online — $40
4. [ ] Save the Certificate of Organization once approved
5. [ ] Apply for EIN (IRS.gov — free, instant) → see [EIN & Business Banking](ein-and-banking.md)
6. [ ] Draft Operating Agreement → see [Operating Agreement](operating-agreement.md)
7. [ ] Open business bank account
8. [ ] Check county occupational license requirement for your county
9. [ ] Register for Kentucky LLET (Limited Liability Entity Tax) — see [Annual Compliance Calendar](../finance/annual-compliance.md)
