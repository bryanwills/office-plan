# EIN & Business Banking

## EIN (Employer Identification Number)

- Apply directly at [IRS.gov EIN Assistant](https://www.irs.gov/businesses/small-businesses-self-employed/apply-for-an-employer-identification-number-ein-online) — **free**, instant issuance online
- Requires the LLC to already be filed/approved with the state first
- Single-member LLC default federal tax treatment = disregarded entity (taxed as sole proprietorship, Schedule C) unless an S-corp/C-corp election is made later
- **Do not pay a third party for this** — several sites charge $50–150 to "get" an EIN that the IRS issues for free

## Business bank account

Opening a dedicated business account is what actually protects the liability separation an LLC provides — commingling personal and business funds is one of the fastest ways to lose that protection ("piercing the corporate veil").

Needed to open:
- [ ] Certificate of Organization (from KY SOS)

## Business credit building (optional, longer-term)

- Business credit card (requires EIN + sometimes a business bank account first)
- NET-30 vendor trade lines (many equipment/supply vendors offer these once you have an EIN and business name on file)
- Not required to start — worth revisiting once initial equipment purchases and banking are settled
