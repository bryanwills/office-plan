# Self-Employment & Disability Resources

State and federal resources relevant to funding/supporting a solo, bootstrapped LLC startup.

## Kentucky Office of Vocational Rehabilitation (KYOVR) — Self-Employment Services

See full details in [Tax Notes](../finance/tax-notes.md#kentucky-vocational-rehabilitation--self-employment-services). Can fund startup equipment and business plan development for Kentucky residents with disabilities pursuing self-employment, including Autism Spectrum Disorder specifically (served through the Perkins Center programs).

- Coordinator: Betty Whitaker — 502-782-3435
- Central office: 502-564-4440
- **Note:** Order of Selection in effect since May 2025 — call to check current intake status before assuming availability.
